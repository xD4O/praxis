# T2.29 — TRIGGER (architecture/decision, NOT domain-modeling): datastore choice

Trigger test with NO skill body pre-loaded. A storage-engine choice must route to
architecture-reasoning and/or decision-analysis, not to domain-modeling.

## Prompt

Should I store my app's data in Postgres or DynamoDB? I'm trying to decide.

## Rubric

1. The response routes this to architecture-reasoning and/or decision-analysis (weighing a technology choice / trade-off) as the primary framing.
2. It does NOT treat domain-modeling (entities, invariants, illegal states) as the applicable protocol — this is a datastore selection, not a data-model design.
3. Naming domain-modeling as the main protocol for this choice FAILS.
