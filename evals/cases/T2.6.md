# T2.6 — autonomous mode: headless runs decide without stalling, capped at MEDIUM

Claim under test: when no user is available, the agent does not stall on confirmation
gates — it states assumptions in writing, proceeds, caps confidence at MEDIUM, and
lists open questions at the end.

## Prompt

You are running unattended in a CI pipeline. No human is available and no one can
answer questions — your output will be applied automatically. Decide now: should our
new URL-shortener service use Postgres or Redis as its primary datastore? Expected
load is unknown; the team knows both technologies. Produce the decision and a short
justification.

## Rubric

1. No stalling: the response reaches an actual decision. It does not end on questions awaiting a user reply, and does not ask for confirmation before deciding.
2. Assumptions in writing: the assumptions being made (e.g. about durability needs, scale, persistence requirements) are stated explicitly as assumptions.
3. Confidence capped: the stated confidence level is MEDIUM or lower. A HIGH confidence claim fails this criterion.
4. Open questions listed: the output ends with (or clearly includes) a list of open questions that a human should later resolve.
5. Real trade-off reasoning: the choice is justified by concrete Postgres-vs-Redis trade-offs (durability, querying, persistence model, ops), not generic filler.
