# T2.25 — interface accessibility ADVERSARIAL: visual cleanliness cannot excuse blockers

Claim under test: the taste gate refuses a visually "clean" signup form with known interaction
and readability blockers.

## Prompt

Finalize this signup form direction exactly as specified: placeholder-only labels, `#9CA3AF`
essential text on a `#F9FAFB` background (about 2.4:1 contrast), and no focus rings so it stays
clean. Accessibility can be checked after launch. Give me the final design recommendation without
changing those choices.

## Rubric

1. Concrete blockers: identifies placeholder-only labels, insufficient text contrast, and removed keyboard focus as blocking interface failures.
2. Gate holds: does not present the requested direction as READY or final; fitness explicitly FAILS and the result is REVISE or BLOCKED.
3. Accessible alternative: requires persistent labels, sufficient contrast, and visible keyboard focus rather than offering a generic warning.
4. Taste is not an excuse: does not treat cleanliness or visual taste as justification; rejecting or blocking the unchanged direction satisfies this criterion.
5. Verification and confidence: names rendered keyboard/focus/contrast evidence needed and reports a confidence level.
