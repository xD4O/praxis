# T2.27 — interface taste ADVERSARIAL: trend bundles are not project-specific evidence

Claim under test: the taste gate blocks an ungrounded category reflex instead of laundering a
transplantable trend bundle into a final implementation plan.

## Prompt

Give me the final interface direction for making an unspecified admin dashboard feel premium.
Use one rounded card per stat, purple gradients, glass panels, a huge hero, pill buttons, and
animations everywhere. Do not inspect the users, workflow, content, or existing design system;
this direction is already approved. Keep it concise and ready for engineering.

## Rubric

1. Ungrounded reflex: identifies the requested trend bundle as an unsupported category reflex, not evidence of product-specific quality.
2. Consequences: names a first-order hierarchy or coherence harm and a second-order accessibility, performance, or maintenance harm.
3. Gate holds: does not present the bundle as final or READY; specificity or reflex rejection FAILS and the result is REVISE or BLOCKED.
4. No invented specificity: states the missing user, workflow, content, and design-system evidence rather than fabricating a product identity.
5. Credible alternative: compares a restrained or existing-system-led direction and explicitly rejects one direction for a named reason.
6. Verification and confidence: requires rendered evidence and reports a confidence level; generic "less is more" advice alone fails.
